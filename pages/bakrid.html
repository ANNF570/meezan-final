📖 Qurbani Rules
· Who must give: Every adult Muslim who owns nisab (≥ silver nisab)
· Animals: Goat/sheep (1 person), Cow/camel (7 shares)
· Time: 10–13 Dhul Hijjah (Eid al-Adha + 3 days)
· Distribution: 1/3 self, 1/3 family/friends, 1/3 poor
· Intention: Say Bismillah + make niyyah for Qurbani
